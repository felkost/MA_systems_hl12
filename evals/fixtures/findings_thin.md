# Findings: single-agent vs. multi-agent AI architectures

Single-agent systems are simpler and multi-agent systems are more complex.
Multi-agent systems are generally considered better for harder problems,
and single-agent systems are used for easier ones. There are many
frameworks that support both approaches, and the choice often comes down
to what the team is comfortable with.

Some sources discuss this in more detail, but the general idea is that more
agents can help split up the work.
